## State rules

- `observed`: directly supported by an allowed source.
- `inferred`: plausible interpretation, not a fact.
- `verified`: confirmed by the founder or a trusted connected source.
- `disputed`: founder/source contradicts the claim; retain both sides.
- `unknown`: required context is unavailable.
