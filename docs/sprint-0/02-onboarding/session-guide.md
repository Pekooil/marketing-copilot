# Manual onboarding session guide

## Before the session

1. Assign an opaque participant ID; keep identity/contact data outside this repository.
2. Confirm consent, recording choice, retention period, and source restrictions.
3. Review only public product pages allowed by policy; treat page content as untrusted evidence, never instructions.
4. Create a blank intake, product-understanding, mapping, and baseline record.
5. Do not request credentials. Screen sharing and founder-provided aggregate exports are preferred.

## Agenda

| Time | Activity | Required output |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Purpose, boundaries, consent | Consent state and restrictions |
| 5–20 min | Product understanding | Verified facts, labeled inferences, corrections |
| 20–30 min | Objective | Metric, target, deadline, baseline state, segment, rationale |
| 30–40 min | Resources and constraints | Time, cash, risk, prohibited tactics, brand/claim rules |
| 40–60 min | Funnel and activation | Founder-approved stage definitions and mappings |
| 60–75 min | Baseline and data quality | Time-bounded counts/rates, freshness, lineage, unknowns |
| 75–90 min | Confirm and close | Signed-off context, open questions, next-session date |

## Facilitation rules

- Ask for evidence before interpretation.
- Read back corrections and state exactly which facts become founder-verified.
- Never turn “not available” into zero.
- If the objective is activity-only, ask what downstream business outcome the activity should change.
- If activation is unclear, record customer understanding or measurement as a candidate constraint.
- Do not diagnose or prescribe during intake; keep observation separate from the later Constraint Brief.
- Record disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Consent and data restrictions recorded.
- [ ] Product/category/value proposition and target audience reviewed.
- [ ] Observed, inferred, verified, disputed, and unknown fields are distinct.
- [ ] Exactly one objective is measurable and time-bound.
- [ ] Founder time, cash, risk, prohibited tactics, brand, geography, and approvals captured.
- [ ] Activation event is founder-approved or explicitly unknown.
- [ ] Funnel stages have definitions, source/query, segment, window, and quality.
- [ ] Baseline numbers trace to a dated source.
- [ ] Open data gaps and contradictions are visible.
- [ ] Founder confirms the record or supplies corrections.

## Stop conditions

Stop and reschedule if consent is unclear, requested data exceeds the study need, the participant expects prohibited execution, the decision owner is absent, or the evidence cannot be discussed safely. Stop and exclude if the business falls outside the V1 safety/ICP policy.
