## Priority factors

`priority = (expected_goal_impact × confidence × learning_value × reversibility) / (founder_hours + cash_cost_weight + time_to_learning_weight + risk_weight)`

Record normalized factors and rationale. Display the score as a ranking aid, never a probability of success.

## Completeness gate

An experiment cannot be approved without a hypothesis, target, exposure, primary metric, duration/review date, decision rule, effort/cost, owner, and instrumentation state. A material post-approval edit creates a new version.
