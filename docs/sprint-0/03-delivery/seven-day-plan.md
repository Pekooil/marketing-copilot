## Portfolio rules

- Totals remain within time and cash unless the founder explicitly overrides.
- Avoid experiments that make the same metric uninterpretable unless jointly designed.
- Do not scale acquisition while severe activation/retention remains unresolved without explicit rationale.
- Reserve learning capacity when confidence is low.
- Nothing public, financial, product-changing, or account-changing is executed by the study.
