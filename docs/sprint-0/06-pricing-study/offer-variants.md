# Offer and message variants

## Positioning language A — AI Growth Lead

“An AI Growth Lead that identifies your current growth bottleneck, prepares measurable experiments, and changes the next plan as evidence comes in.”

## Positioning language B — adaptive weekly growth plan

“A continuously adapting weekly growth plan grounded in your product, funnel, constraints, experiments, and prior learnings.”

Use one primary phrase per participant before asking which is clearer; do not show both first and invite preference bias.

## Price cells

### $49/month founder offer

- One workspace and primary objective
- Manual metrics/CSV
- Weekly constraint brief and plan
- Experiment ledger and basic Growth Memory
- Preparation-only assets within a stated allowance

### $99/month founder offer

- Everything in $49
- One validated read-only analytics connection when available
- Daily exception summary
- More preparation runs/assets
- Richer evidence and memory inspection

### $199/month growth offer

- Everything in $99
- Collaboration for a small team
- Revenue/search connections when supported
- Advanced measurement and higher research/preparation allowance
- Still no autonomous sending, publishing, spending, or deployment in V1

Feature packaging is a hypothesis. Keep the core learning loop available in every paid cell so the price test does not become three different products.

## Free diagnostic hypothesis

Test separately: a free, evidence-limited company/funnel assessment followed by paid live connections, experiments, weekly adaptation, and memory. Make clear what evidence is missing and never use a false full diagnosis as a teaser.

## Claims prohibited in pricing copy

- Guaranteed growth/revenue or outcome-based savings.
- “Fully autonomous marketing team” for V1.
- Perfect attribution or causal certainty.
- Unlimited work when budgets/limits exist.
- Scarcity, discounts, or participant counts not actually true.
