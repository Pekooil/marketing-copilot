# Cohort specification and quotas

## Recruitment question

Can a founder with a launched, measurable software product and no growth lead use an evidence-backed constraint and experiment loop well enough to return for a second weekly plan?

## Required inclusion criteria

A candidate must:

1. Be a founder/owner or the person directly responsible for growth decisions.
2. Operate a launched SaaS, app, browser extension, developer tool, or AI product.
3. Have a short digital funnel that can reasonably map to visit → signup/install → activation → retained use → paid, even if some stages are currently unknown.
4. Have no full-time growth leader and no mature channel/data team.
5. Have at least some live users, traffic, interviews, or recurring qualitative feedback.
6. Be willing to define a measurable objective and founder-approved activation event.
7. Be able to spend 60–90 minutes on onboarding and 30–45 minutes per weekly review for two weeks.
8. Be willing to run or seriously assess one bounded experiment; the study never executes external actions for them.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude:

- pre-launch ideas without users or a measurable surface;
- ecommerce catalogs, local/offline businesses, marketplaces with unresolved two-sided attribution, political campaigns, and regulated lead-generation businesses;
- large marketing organizations or agencies managing unrelated client workspaces;
- enterprise products whose main conversion takes months and cannot yield a two-week learning;
- candidates seeking mass cold outreach, fake engagement, content farms, ad arbitrage, or autonomous spending/publishing;
- anyone unwilling to share even aggregate funnel counts or qualitative customer evidence.

## Target mix for 8–12 participants

| Dimension | Desired quota | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Product type: B2B SaaS/developer tool | 3–5 | Core self-serve beachhead |
| Product type: prosumer/app/extension | 3–5 | Tests adjacent short funnels |
| Other qualifying AI/software product | 1–2 | Avoids overfitting one product form |
| Analytics: PostHog | At least 2 if available | Tests proposed first connector demand |
| Analytics: GA4 or Mixpanel | At least 2 | Measures alternative prevalence |
| Analytics: none/manual only | At least 2 | Tests manual fallback and data burden |
| Stage: pre-revenue | 2–4 | Tests sparse revenue signals |
| Stage: revenue to ~$20K MRR | 4–7 | Tests measurable operating loops |
| Founder time: ≤5 hours/week | At least 4 | Tests constrained portfolio value |

Quotas are directional. Do not force-fit unqualified participants merely to fill a cell.

## Recruitment sources to use later

Use opt-in or relationship-based sources first:

- founder’s existing founder network and portfolio/community contacts;
- users who explicitly opted into product research;
- technical-founder communities where study recruitment is permitted;
- accelerators, coworking groups, and builder communities through an approved organizer;
- public study-recruitment posts that invite inbound participation.

Do not scrape individuals or send unsolicited mass outreach.

## Replacement and attrition rules

- Recruit 10–12 to retain at least eight usable onboarding cases.
- Keep a waitlist of up to four qualified candidates.
- Replace a participant only when they withdraw, miss two scheduled sessions, reveal an exclusion condition, or cannot provide any decision-relevant evidence.
- Preserve partial records and mark the reason; do not delete drop-off because it is product evidence.

## Success metrics

- Qualified-to-enrolled rate.
- Enrolled-to-onboarding-complete rate.
- Percentage committing to two loops.
- Distribution by analytics platform and funnel maturity.
- Recruitment source by subsequent completion, not lead volume.
