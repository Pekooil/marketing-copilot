## Copy review checklist

- No promise of growth outcomes or causal certainty.
- No fake urgency, scarcity, or participant count.
- Time commitment and manual nature are explicit.
- External actions are explicitly excluded.
- Compensation is mentioned only after the founder defines it.
- Use only channels that permit study recruitment; record opt-out immediately.
