# Recruitment screener

**Target completion time:** Under five minutes  
**Disposition:** `qualify`, `manual review`, or `exclude`

## Intro shown to candidates

We are studying a manual “AI Growth Lead” workflow for technical founders with launched software products. The study helps you identify a current growth bottleneck, define one-to-three measurable experiments, and review what the evidence changes the following week. This is a research prototype, not an autonomous marketing service, and it will not publish, send, spend, deploy, or change your accounts.

## Questions

1. What is your role?
   - Founder/owner
   - Growth/marketing owner at a 2–5 person team
   - Engineer/product owner responsible for growth decisions
   - Other

2. Which best describes your product?
   - B2B SaaS
   - Prosumer SaaS/app
   - Browser extension
   - Developer tool/API
   - AI software product
   - Ecommerce/local service/marketplace/other

3. Is the product live and usable by real users today?
   - Yes
   - Private beta with active users
   - No

4. Roughly what stage is the business?
   - Pre-revenue with active users
   - $1–$1,000 MRR
   - $1,001–$5,000 MRR
   - $5,001–$20,000 MRR
   - Above $20,000 MRR
   - Prefer not to say

5. Who currently owns growth strategy?
   - Me, alongside product/engineering
   - A generalist teammate
   - A full-time growth leader/team
   - An agency
   - Nobody consistently

6. Which analytics source best reflects product use?
   - PostHog
   - GA4
   - Mixpanel
   - Amplitude
   - Another platform
   - Database/manual spreadsheet
   - No reliable analytics

7. Can you identify at least one event that means a user received meaningful value?
   - Yes, and it is tracked
   - Yes, but it is not reliably tracked
   - Unsure
   - No

8. What is your most important measurable growth goal for the next 1–3 months?  
   Short text. Manual review rejects activity-only goals unless the founder is willing to translate them into an outcome.

9. How much founder/team time can you realistically spend on growth each week?
   - Under 2 hours
   - 2–5 hours
   - 6–10 hours
   - More than 10 hours

10. Can you join one 60–90 minute onboarding session and two 30–45 minute weekly reviews?
    - Yes
    - Maybe; scheduling needed
    - No

11. Are you willing to share aggregate funnel counts or qualitative customer evidence for the study?
    - Yes
    - Yes, with specified exclusions
    - No

12. What tactics must never be recommended for your company?  
    Short text; this informs safety and resource constraints.

## Disposition rules

### Automatically qualify for scheduling

- Q1 is decision owner;
- Q2 is an included software type;
- Q3 is live/private beta with users;
- Q5 has no full-time growth leader/team;
- Q10 is yes;
- Q11 permits aggregate or qualitative evidence.

### Manual review

- goal is vague but plausibly measurable;
- activation is unknown;
- analytics is absent;
- agency support exists but founder still owns the decision;
- revenue is above the target band but the team still behaves like the ICP.

### Exclude

- product is not live;
- candidate does not own growth decisions;
- full-time growth/data organization exists;
- no usable evidence can be shared;
- candidate cannot complete the two-loop cadence;
- requested tactics violate the study’s anti-spam or safety boundaries.

Never infer sensitive traits or use them in disposition.
