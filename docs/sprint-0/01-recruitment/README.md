## Definition of prepared

- Inclusion, exclusion, diversity, and replacement rules are explicit.
- The screener can be completed in under five minutes.
- Outreach states the time commitment and that the product is a manual concierge study.
- No compensation, privacy, or outcome claim is invented.
- Candidate disposition and consent are auditable without storing unnecessary sensitive data.

## Evidence still required

- 8–12 consented, qualified founders enrolled.
- Distribution across analytics maturity and product types meets the quota.
- At least five agree to the full two-loop commitment.
