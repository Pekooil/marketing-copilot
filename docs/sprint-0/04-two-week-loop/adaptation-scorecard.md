- New evidence received:

- Actual constraint/plan change:
- Required adaptation sentence present:
- Relevant prior failure retrieved:

## Product signals

- Time to first credible brief
- Onboarding-to-experiment approval
- Experiment launch and conclusion
- Plan acceptance/edit/rejection/defer
- Percentage of second plans citing prior learning
- Repeated recommendation after negative learning
- Founder-rated trust, specificity, and time saved
- Requested continuation and stated willingness to pay
