# Founder feedback and decision log

Record feedback immediately after each major artifact and at study close.

## Artifact decision

- Artifact/type/version:
- Viewed at:
- Decision: accept / edit / reject / defer / override / request evidence
- Founder reason in their words:
- Confidence before/after viewing:
- Time required to review:
- What felt generic, unsupported, repetitive, or surprising:
- What would have made the decision easier:
- Preference or evidence conflict:
- Downstream plan effect:

Rejection is preference/decision evidence, not automatically an experiment failure.

## Weekly interview prompts

1. Was the named constraint credible? What was missing or overstated?
2. Did the plan reduce decision burden, or merely add tasks?
3. Which proposal did you actually launch and why?
4. What prevented execution of the others?
5. Could a fresh generic AI chat have produced this output?
6. What prior context or learning did the second plan use correctly?
7. What did the workflow forget or repeat?
8. Did the evidence explanation increase or reduce trust?
9. Would you return next week without a reminder?
10. What would you pay to continue after a completed learning loop?

## Study close ratings

Use 1–5 plus explanation:

- Constraint credibility
- Recommendation specificity
- Evidence trust
- Feasibility within resources
- Time saved
- Memory/continuity value
- Likelihood to continue weekly
- Discomfort/surprise from any proposed action
