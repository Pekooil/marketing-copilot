# Two-week operating cadence

## Day 0 — onboarding and first plan

- Confirm product context, objective, resources, funnel, baseline, and data quality.
- Produce candidate scores and a reviewed Constraint Brief.
- Record founder confirmation/override.
- Propose no more than three pre-registered experiments.
- Record individual decisions; approved items enter `planned`, not `running`.

## Days 1–6 — execution support and exceptions

- Founder performs any public, product, or account action.
- Operator prepares only reversible assets and instructions.
- Record instrumentation readiness, launch/completion evidence, exposure, and protocol deviations.
- Review only meaningful exceptions: stale/conflicted data, guardrail risk, milestone, founder question, or decisive new evidence.
- On no-change days record: “No strategic change; continue collecting data.”

## Day 7 — weekly review 1

- Close, continue, stop, or invalidate experiments using pre-registered rules.
- Separate measured facts, likely explanations, and unknowns.
- Create learning candidates only from valid measurement reports.
- Recompute constraint candidates and compare allocation.
- Produce the second plan with the required adaptation sentence.
- Record complete-plan and per-experiment decisions.

## Days 8–13 — second execution/evidence cycle

Repeat the exception process. Watch specifically for whether prior feedback and learning make guidance more specific and less repetitive.

## Day 14 — weekly review 2 and study close

- Review goal/funnel movement and every experiment state.
- Record biggest learning, biggest unknown, and invalidity incidents.
- Test whether the system would repeat a failed strategy without new rationale.
- Capture founder usefulness, trust, time saved, willingness to continue/pay, and preferred next artifact.
- Do not attribute business outcomes causally beyond the protocol’s evidence.

## Operator service levels

- Acknowledge founder input within the agreed study window.
- Never manufacture daily advice.
- Surface stale/conflicted data before strategy.
- Version every material protocol/plan change.
- Keep one canonical narrative; specialist notes remain supporting artifacts.
