# S1-010 resource and policy constraints

Each objective receives an immutable, versioned operating envelope: founder minutes per week, integer minor-unit cash and ISO currency, risk tolerance, prohibited tactics, brand/claim rules, audience/geography limits, and approval preferences.

Time and cash cannot be negative. Currency is three uppercase letters, and tactic/rule lists reject blank or case-insensitive duplicate entries. Analytics may emit only changed-field categories.

The global V1 action policy always wins: Class A reads, Class B internal writes, and Class C reversible preparation may proceed within authorization; Class D sending, Class E publishing/spending/deployment, and Class F sensitive or irreversible actions are denied even if a stored preference asks for them.
